# NexaStream Setup Guides

Device-specific setup instructions for installing NexaStream on your device.

## Supported Devices

- [Amazon Fire TV / Fire Stick](./firetv.md)
- [Android TV / Smartphone / Box](./android.md)
- [iPhone / iPad / Apple TV](./ios.md)
- [Windows & macOS](./windows.md)
- [Roku TV & Streaming Stick](./roku.md)

## Quick Setup Overview

1. **Choose Your Device** - Select from the list above
2. **Get an IPTV Player** - Download a compatible player for your device
3. **Add Your Account** - Enter your NexaStream credentials
4. **Start Streaming** - Enjoy 20,000+ channels and 180,000+ movies & series
