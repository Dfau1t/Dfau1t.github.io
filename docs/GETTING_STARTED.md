# Getting Started with NexaStream

Welcome to NexaStream! This guide will help you get up and running in just a few minutes.

## Step 1: Create Your Account

1. Visit [nexa-stream.live](https://nexa-stream.live)
2. Click "**Kostenlos testen**" (Try for Free) to start your 24-hour free trial
3. Choose your preferred payment method:
   - PayPal
   - Bank Transfer
   - Cryptocurrency

## Step 2: Choose Your Device

Select your device type from the list below and follow the device-specific setup guide:

- [Amazon Fire TV / Fire Stick](./docs/setup/firetv.md)
- [Android TV / Smartphone / Box](./docs/setup/android.md)
- [iPhone / iPad / Apple TV](./docs/setup/ios.md)
- [Windows & macOS](./docs/setup/windows.md)
- [Roku TV](./docs/setup/roku.md)

## Step 3: Install a Player

Recommended IPTV players:

**For Android:**
- TiviMate IPTV Player
- Televizo IPTV Player
- Smarters Player

**For iOS:**
- Smarters Player Lite
- Infuse

**For Desktop:**
- Hot Player
- IBO Player Pro
- VLC Media Player

**For Fire TV:**
- Hot Player
- IBO Player Pro

## Step 4: Add Your Credentials

Once you have your account and player installed:

1. Open your IPTV player
2. Select "Add Playlist" or "Add Source"
3. Enter your NexaStream credentials:
   - **Server URL**: Your unique server address
   - **Username**: Your account username
   - **Password**: Your account password
4. Save and wait for channels to load

## Step 5: Start Streaming!

Your channels should now be available. Browse and enjoy 20,000+ live TV channels and 180,000+ movies & series.

## Troubleshooting

### Channels Not Loading?

1. Check your internet connection (minimum 5 Mbps recommended)
2. Verify your credentials are correct
3. Restart the player application
4. Contact support via WhatsApp or live chat

### Buffering or Freezing?

1. Check your internet speed
2. Try a lower quality stream (if available)
3. Restart your device
4. Close other apps using bandwidth

### Account Issues?

- Forgotten password? Contact our support team
- Connection limit exceeded? Upgrade to a plan with more concurrent streams
- Billing questions? We're here to help 24/7

## FAQ

### How many devices can I use simultaneously?

Each plan includes a set number of concurrent streams. Our Starter plan includes 1 connection, Premium includes 2, and Diamond includes 4.

### Is there a trial period?

Yes! Enjoy a free 24-hour trial to test all features with full access to our complete channel and VOD library.

### What if I'm not satisfied?

We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee if you're not completely satisfied with NexaStream.

### Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely! All subscriptions are non-binding. Cancel anytime through your account dashboard.
